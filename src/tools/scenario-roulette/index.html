🎲 WRO 2026 Roulette

Future Engineers · Self-Driving Cars

Draws a physically buildable track scenario following the official Appendix A procedure (coin tosses, a die and the 36-card deck), and keeps a log of your Open Challenge attempts so you can see how your score improves with each robot model.

How the draw follows the rulebook: the field is drawn to scale from the official 2026 playfield file (section lines at 1000 / 1500 / 2000 mm, seat rows at 400 and 600 mm from the outer wall, orange/blue corner lines). Checked against the figures: start section and single-sign section by the coin pairs of Figures 7a / 8b (heads-heads = 1, tails-heads = 7, heads-tails = 3, tails-tails = 5); corridor widths tossed from the start section clockwise (Figure 7b); start zones as in Figure 7c, where zones 1 and 4 touch the inner wall and are re-rolled on a narrow start straight; the single sign on the middle seat of the outer row, which is exactly why card 9 or 10 (the same seat) is removed; one card for each of the other 3 straights, laid with its thick line on the inner wall; the parking lot against the outer wall starting at the section line where the clockwise direction enters (Figure 8d), with that section's pillars moved to the inner row (Figure 8e). Only the driving-direction draw isn't described, so it's a coin toss. National organizers can adapt these rules.
