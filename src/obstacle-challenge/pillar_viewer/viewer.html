Pillars are only searched in the band between the yellow line (ignore top) and the red NEAR line (counted in rows up from the bottom edge); drag either line on the picture. Side zone: a pillar whose centre is within ± this many pixels of the middle is CENTER. A red pillar is on the correct side once it is left of the zone, a green one once it is right of it: green halo = correct side, red halo = not yet.

The algorithm, step by step

1Picture

160×120 RGB565 from the camera, read with readPixel() (mirror / flip applied).

2Colors

Every pixel to HSV and classify(): red, green or nothing.

3Blobs

BFS joins touching pixels of one color. Under MIN_PIXELS is noise (grey dashes).

4Closest

frontPillarOfBlob() keeps the front part, isCloser() picks the lowest bottom.
